What "training" looks like

        
Each epoch the model predicts, measures its error, computes gradients and moves its weights a little. The decision boundary moves, the loss curve falls. Change the learning rate to see the two classic failure modes: crawling (too small) and bouncing or exploding (too large). Switch to the circle dataset to see why a straight-line model needs a hidden layer.
