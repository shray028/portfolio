Why requirements for ML are differentKey points for the exam

Probabilistic

Traditional software: input + code → deterministic output. ML: data + expected output → learned model. Requirements must state acceptable error (e.g. recall ≥ 0.9), not exact behaviour.

Soft goals

Non-functional requirements such as interpretability, latency (< 100 ms), robustness to missing values and accuracy are modelled as soft goals that are satisficed, not fully met.

Trade-offs

Influence links (+ / −) make conflicts explicit: an SVM may raise accuracy but hurt interpretability — essential in regulated domains like lending or healthcare.

Attribute scores are illustrative teaching values, not benchmark results. GR4ML was proposed by Nalchigar & Yu (University of Toronto).
