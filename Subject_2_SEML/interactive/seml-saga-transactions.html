Why not a distributed ACID transaction?Key exam points

Database per service

Each microservice owns its data; no other service touches its tables. That independence rules out a single shared ACID transaction.

2PC doesn't scale

Two-phase commit holds locks across network boundaries and blocks if the coordinator fails — poor availability for microservices.

Eventual consistency

A saga is a sequence of local transactions. Intermediate states are visible, so the system is eventually — not immediately — consistent.
