Rolling / ramped
Pods are replaced incrementally (4:0 → 3:1 → 2:2 → 1:3 → 0:4) with maxSurge/maxUnavailable limits. No extra environment needed and no downtime, but v1 and v2 serve traffic at the same time, and rollback is another rolling update.

        
Blue-green
Two identical environments: blue (live, v1) and green (v2). Test green, then flip the router — instant switch and instant rollback. Costs double the capacity during the release.
