How to answer so it sounds senior

Concept questions

  1. One-line definition in plain words.
  2. Intuition or a tiny example (a picture you could draw).
  3. Formula or mechanism if relevant.
  4. When it breaks / trade-offs.
  5. What you'd do in practice, ideally from your own work.

"Bias is being consistently wrong, variance is being inconsistently right…"

"It broke in production" questions

  1. Localise: data, pipeline, model, serving or experiment?
  2. Cheapest checks first: bugs, logging, recent deploys.
  3. Hypotheses ranked by likelihood, each with the evidence that would confirm it.
  4. Fix with the smallest change.
  5. Prevent: a monitor, a test, an eval case.

Design questions

  1. Clarify goal, users, scale, latency, constraints.
  2. Metrics: offline, online, guardrails.
  3. Data and labels (and their biases).
  4. Baseline v1 → stronger model.
  5. Serving architecture and fallbacks.
  6. Experiment, monitoring, iteration.

Narrate trade-offs; draw the boxes.

"Tell me about a project"

  1. Problem and why it mattered (a number).
  2. Your role, specifically.
  3. Data, labels, baseline.
  4. Approach and what failed first.
  5. How you proved it worked (offline and online).
  6. Impact, and what you'd do differently.
